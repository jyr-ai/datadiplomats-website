The short version.

We collect only what we need to respond to your inquiry, send the updates you ask for, run the optional Google Workspace tools, and understand how our site is used. We do not sell personal information. We do not ask visitors to submit beneficiary, client, health, or other sensitive personal data through this website. Where we use Google advertising or analytics, we tell you below and explain how to opt out.

This Privacy Policy explains how Data Diplomats for Nonprofits, Inc. (“Data Diplomats,” “we,” “us”), a nonprofit organization based in New York City, handles personal information collected through this website (the “Site”). It does not govern the data our nonprofit partners process in the AI systems we build with them; that is covered by each engagement agreement and our AI Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact / discovery requests: name, email, organization, organization type, sector, AI maturity stage, and any message you write.
  • Newsletter (“Civic AI Briefing”): your email address.
  • Correspondence: anything you send us by email or during a call.

Please do not include personal details about the people your organization serves (for example names, health, immigration, or financial information) in any form or message on the Site.

Information from Google sign-in (optional)

The Site offers an optional “Sign in with Google” feature that powers our Google Sheets workspace tools. If you use it, we receive your basic Google profile (name, email address, profile picture) and, with your consent, access to Google Drive and Google Sheets files as described in Section 4.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, and date/time of visits.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 3.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule discovery calls, and scope engagements.
  • To send the newsletter and event updates you requested. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To operate the Google Workspace tools you choose to use.
  • To keep the Site secure, prevent abuse, and fix technical problems.
  • To measure Site performance and the effectiveness of our outreach, including our Google Ads campaigns.
  • To meet legal, accounting, and tax-exempt reporting obligations.

We do not use personal information from the Site to train AI models, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Where supported by your browser, we also honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of “sale” or “sharing” for advertising.

4. Google user data (Google API Services)

If you choose to sign in with Google and connect Google Workspace, we request access to Google Drive and Google Sheets so that you can create, open, read, and update spreadsheets (for example, intake trackers and AI blueprint workbooks) from the Site.

  • What we access: your basic profile and the Drive and Sheets files needed to perform the action you request.
  • How we use it: only to provide and improve the user-facing spreadsheet features you request within the Site. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • Storage: the access token is held in your browser’s memory for the current session only. We do not write it to persistent browser storage or store your Google files or their contents on our servers.
  • Sharing: we do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the feature you requested, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to you.
  • Humans: our staff do not read your Google data unless you give us explicit permission for a specific purpose (such as support), it is necessary for security or to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Revoking access: you can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data Diplomats’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising except for the Google cookies described in Section 3. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that host the Site, send email, manage our contact records, and provide analytics and advertising measurement (including Google LLC and Firebase), under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • Authorities or advisers where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • A successor organization in a merger or restructuring, with notice to you.

6. Retention

  • Inquiry and correspondence records: up to 3 years after our last contact, unless an engagement begins.
  • Newsletter addresses: until you unsubscribe.
  • Analytics and advertising data: according to the retention settings configured in those tools (up to 14 months for analytics).
  • Records we must keep for legal or tax purposes: for the legally required period.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege practices to protect information. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your information has been compromised, contact us right away. To report a security vulnerability, email security@datadiplomats.org.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not discriminate against people who exercise these rights. To make a request, email privacy@datadiplomats.org; we may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within 45 days (30 days for EEA/UK requests). You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority or, in the U.S., your state attorney general.

  • California and other U.S. states: we have not sold personal information in the past 12 months. Personal information categories we collect are identifiers, internet activity, and professional/organizational information, as described above.
  • EEA/UK: our legal bases are consent (cookies, newsletter), legitimate interests (responding to inquiries, security, and measuring outreach), and legal obligation. Our servers and service providers may be in the United States; where required, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Children

The Site is intended for nonprofit professionals and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children and do not use it for advertising. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

The Site may link to other websites (for example, event pages and partner organizations). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change is material, we will provide a more prominent notice or, where required, ask for your consent again.
